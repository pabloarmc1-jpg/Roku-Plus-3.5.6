ROKU PLUS 3.5.0

Continue Watching / playback-state reliability update

- Preserve provider identity fields when progress updates arrive with a reduced or incomplete item object.
- Preserve previously saved provider, providerId, providerSlug, providerTmdbId, providerKind, episode/season identity, IMDb ID, and display metadata during record updates.
- This is intended to prevent CineHax and SeriesMetro restore metadata from being erased during playback progress updates, and to protect CineCalidad slug identity.
- No interface redesign or player layout changes.

Validation note: source-level changes only; not compiled with the Roku SDK or tested on a physical Roku device in this environment.
