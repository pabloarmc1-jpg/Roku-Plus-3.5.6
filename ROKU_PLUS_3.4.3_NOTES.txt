Roku Plus 3.4.3

Crash fix based on the user's Roku BrightScript Micro Debugger trace:
- PlusSceneViews.brs detailScreen() no longer compares an absent/non-numeric item.runtime directly to zero. It normalizes the optional runtime with rpWatchNumber() before rendering.
- The Continue Watching rail also normalizes optional duration and position values before calculating progress, preventing malformed or incomplete watch-state values from crashing the render thread.
- The existing visual design and navigation behavior are otherwise unchanged.

Validation performed:
- ZIP integrity check and XML parsing of packaged component files.
- Not compiled with Roku SDK and not physically tested on a Roku device.

Keyboard Back issue remains under investigation and needs its own debugger trace after reproducing the keyboard close behavior.
